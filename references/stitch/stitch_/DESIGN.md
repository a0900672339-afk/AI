## Brand & Style

This design system embodies contemporary minimalist Chinese aesthetics (新中式極簡/雅致留白), pairing the quiet poise of classical ink wash painting with the structural discipline of modern digital portfolio design. Conceived for 2D concept illustrators and digital visual artists, it treats the screen as an absorbent, tactile sheet of aged Xuan paper (*宣紙*) where works of art rest with ample breathing room.

### Key Tenets
- **Intentional Blank Space (*留白 - Liúbái*):** Whitespace is not an absence of content, but a foundational structural asset that creates focus, serenity, and dignity.
- **Ink Wash Restraint (*水墨層次*):** Value relationships emulate variations of pine smoke ink (*松煙墨*), prioritizing charcoal and graphite tones over harsh digital blacks.
- **The Crimson Seal (*朱砂印章*):** Punctual, vibrant hits of cinnabar vermilion anchor interactions, acting as visual signatures akin to artist seals (*落款印*).
- **Architectural Subtlety:** Hairline dividers, understated parchment tints, and translucent vellum surfaces replace heavy drop shadows and ornate framing.

### Emotional Target
The experience must feel meditative, scholarly, and refined—evoking museum curation, archival quality, and exceptional draftsmanship without archaic cliché.

## Colors

The palette derives from physical artist ateliers: unsized rice paper, mineral cinnabar pigment, slate inkstones, and distant mountain glazes.

- **Primary (`#B83B3B` - Cinnabar Vermilion / 硃砂紅):** Used sparingly for focal interaction points, active indicators, notifications, and artist signature stamps. It commands immediate focus against muted backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#4A607A` - Distant Mountain Indigo / 遠山黛藍):** Used for analytical details, metadata chips, technical software skills, secondary CTAs, and quiet hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#D8D2C5` - Washed Ink Tone / 淡墨染緣):** Applied to delicate hairline borders, divider seams, structural grid lines, and interactive card perimeters.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF9F6` base canvas, `#F4F0EA` nested containers):** Mimics the warmth of hand-pressed raw silk and mulberry paper.
- **Neutral Inks (`#202020` Deep Pine Ink for titles, `#333333` for body, `#555555` for auxiliary notations):** Softened carbon blacks that eliminate harsh screen glare while maintaining crisp contrast.

## Typography

Typographic structure balances calligraphic legacy with contemporary digital legibility:
- **Headings (Noto Serif):** Delivers the stately weight of classical Songti woodblock and stone inscriptions. For bilingual or vertical titles, pair with traditional glyph conventions and extended tracking.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** High-clarity, rationalist grotesque ensuring that project descriptions, artwork dimensions, software specifications, and interface labels remain effortlessly scannable across dense art viewports.
- **Vertical Orientation Accents:** Editorial titling may leverage vertical CSS writing modes (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) for thematic section tags or artist mottos, accompanied by an ink-stroke spacer line.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an expansive 12-column grid capable of displaying wide aspect-ratio artwork (matte paintings, character turnarounds) with generous peripheral breathing room.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid with `margin-desktop` (64px) and `gutter-desktop` (40px). Max page width is capped at 1440px to retain intimate gallery framing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 32px margins and 24px gutters. Artwork previews switch from 3-up to 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px margins and 16px gutters. Vertical accent elements reflow into compact horizontal tags above headings.
- **Negative Space Rhythm:** Section containers demand substantial vertical padding (typically `space-xl` multiplied by 2 to 3) to preserve the unhurried pacing of Chinese scroll compositions.

## Elevation & Depth

To remain faithful to flat rice paper and brush-painted planes, traditional drop shadows are strictly avoided. Depth is achieved via:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces step forward by transitioning from the main canvas (`#FAF9F6`) to soft vellum tiles (`#F7F5F0`) and layered inspection panels (`#F4F0EA`).
- **Parchment Glassmorphism:** Elevated bars and floating control panels use translucent paper backing (`rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.82)`) with a high-diffusion blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), allowing artwork tones to softly permeate underneath.
- **Hairline Framing:** Borders remain uniform at 1px thickness in `#E6E1D8` or `#D8D2C5`, lending each modal, preview frame, and tag the crispness of fine-cut mount boards.
- **Modal Depth:** Lightbox overlays use a dense ink veil (`rgba(32, 32, 32, 0.88)`) combined with backdrop blur, isolating single illustrations in gallery focus.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** geometry. Edge radiuses are minimal (2px to 4px) to retain the sharp, precise qualities of cut paper sheets, ink stone slabs, and wooden printing blocks. 

- **Cards and Viewports:** Clipped with subtle 4px radiuses (`rounded-sm`) to soften digital edges while preserving pure compositional rectangularity.
- **Seal Stamps (Monograms & Badges):** Square or slightly rounded square forms (4px radius), evoking stone or soapstone chop chops (*印章*).
- **Interactive Micro-tags:** Controlled at 2px to 4px radiuses; full pill shapes are omitted to avoid conflicting with the architectural serenity.

## Components

### 1. Frosted Paper Navigation Bar
- **Surface:** Fixed header with `rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.85)` fill, `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`, and a 1px border-bottom in `#E6E1D8`.
- **Brand Monogram:** A 36x36px square seal in Cinnabar Vermilion (`#B83B3B`) with a negative-space Hanzi/Latin glyph, enclosed in a 1px inset boundary.
- **Navigation Links:** `label-md` uppercase typography with wide letter spacing; inactive links in `#555555`, active item denoted by a crimson dot (4x4px) centered beneath the label.

### 2. Hero Centerpiece & Vertical Accents
- **Layout:** Asymmetric split. Left/center features high-res illustration focal artwork with hairline border containment; left margin hosts an architectural vertical rubric (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) containing the artist's statement or genre focus in Songti.
- **Action CTAs:** 
  - *Primary Button:* Solid Deep Pine Ink (`#202020`) with white text, 4px border radius, and a 1px vermilion hover underline.
  - *Secondary Button:* Transparent background with 1px `#D8D2C5` border, shifting to `#4A607A` on hover.

### 3. Gallery Grid & Filter Tabs
- **Filter Bar:** Minimal text row with active underline in Vermilion; inactive categories rendered in `#555555`. Count badges displayed as superscripts in `label-sm`.
- **Art Cards:** Clean aspect-ratio preservation with an imperceptible 1px `#E6E1D8` border. On hover, images undergo a subtle zoom (scale 1.02, ease-out 400ms) while an ink wash gradient (`rgba(32, 32, 32, 0.6)`) reveals title, medium, and year in white.
- **Lightbox Modal:** Full-viewport immersive overlay in `#202020` (88% opacity) featuring high-resolution pan/zoom controls, color palette breakdown chips, and artwork conceptual lore in an off-canvas drawer.

### 4. Skills Matrix & Milestone Timeline
- **Artistic Timeline:** A single vertical 1px hair-rule in `#D8D2C5` anchored by Cinnabar diamond nodes (`◆`) for solo exhibitions, game studio milestones, and client publications. Dates styled in `Noto Serif` italic.
- **Software & Technique Matrix:** Grouped under two buckets: Traditional Foundations and Digital Craft. Displayed via discrete, un-shadowed parchment tiles (`#F7F5F0`) with hairline boundaries.

### 5. Contact Inquiries & Social Footnotes
- **Input Fields:** Flush-bottom underline fields (border-bottom: 1px solid `#D8D2C5`) on `#FAF9F6`, shifting to `#B83B3B` on `:focus`. Labels rest in quiet `label-md` grey.
- **Copyable Email Capsule:** Minimal container featuring one-click copy with tactile toast confirmation ("Copied to clipboard / 已複製").
- **Social Badges:** Monochromatic icons (ArtStation, Behance, X, IG) rendered in Deep Ink `#333333`, transitioning to Vermilion `#B83B3B` upon hover.
